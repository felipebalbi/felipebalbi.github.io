## Collected evidence

- Twelve original screenshots, unchanged bytes and original PNG/JPEG formats.
- Ten main workload captures: four initial held-F variants, two awake-idle
  variants, two initial repeated-tap variants, and two pacing-only captures.
- The unpaired initial `both` capture is explicitly excluded from comparisons.
- An early `both` capture before the scripted repeat is retained in the
  inventory but excluded because its workload wasn't recorded.
- `measurements.csv` contains GUI window statistics, not raw PPK2 samples.
- `measurement-summary.json` includes screenshot hashes, calculations,
  owner confirmations, missing metadata, and prepared build provenance.
- `build-records/erase-*` contains the original build records, checksum lists,
  and erase-enabled keyboard configuration for each prepared variant. No
  firmware binaries are bundled, and the exact image flashed per capture
  hasn't been confirmed against those checksums.

## Findings that can be stated now

- Matrix timing changes together reduced held-switch charge by about 52% in
  the first captures. Pacing-only is now measured; settling-only is not.
- Repeated manual taps used 26.65 mC on baseline versus 15.62 mC on matrix over
  10 s: a 41.4% descriptive difference between these captures.
- The owner confirmed approximately matched rhythm/count, unchanged other
  tapping conditions, and every F tap registering.
- Baseline and both awake-idle averages were 968.21 and 975.70 µA respectively:
  no established saving.
- SPIM-only held-F current was close to baseline; both was close to matrix.
  No useful net benefit is demonstrated for the tested workload.
- Pacing-only held F: 3.44 mA, 14.34 mA maximum, 34.42 mC over 10 s.
- Pacing-only tapping F: 2.23 mA, 14.06 mA maximum, 22.33 mC over 10 s.
- The owner confirmed the two new screenshot workload labels. They show
  about 20% and 16% less charge than the earlier held/tapping baselines.
- The combined matrix variant improves further than pacing-only. That
  establishes an additional observed benefit with pacing enabled, not the
  standalone settling effect or absence of an interaction.
- Pacing-only uses the earlier published revisions plus a frozen local patch,
  not the newer upstream cleanup branch. Its record includes the patch and
  preserves all non-RMK dependency versions/sources/checksums.

## Optional follow-up experiments

- A separate 5 ms active-scan gap, preserving the measured 1 ms version.
  Keep settling, debounce, BLE policy and SPIM unchanged. It is only a
  proposal; no such firmware or measurements have been created here.
- Matrix-only and SPIM-only awake-idle captures.
- Short-settling-only, plus repeated pacing-only/combined comparisons, if
  attributing standalone and interaction effects becomes important.
- SPIM under display-heavy workloads, only if worth investigating; don't
  infer continuous clock activity from a retained driver.

No battery-life claim, latency guarantee, per-tap charge, confidence interval,
or 5 ms result is supported by this first set.
